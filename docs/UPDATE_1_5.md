# DEADLANE v1.5 — Graphics Polish & Peripheral Detail

Version 1.5 is a visual-quality pass built on the v1.4 Graphics & World Overhaul. Gameplay rules remain intact: the survivor is manually positioned, there is no zombie auto-targeting, and firing requires holding FIRE (or Space on desktop).

## Character detail

- Replaced the survivor's box-built backpack with a dedicated modeled pack including external pockets, bedroll, straps, buckles and MOLLE-style rows.
- Replaced primitive survivor hands with modeled tactical gloves and readable finger shapes.
- Added modeled zombie hands with asymmetry/broken-finger detail.
- Added a modeled damaged zombie lower jaw with visible tooth geometry.
- Retained the higher-detail v1.3/v1.4 heads, torsos, limbs, boots, zombie-type silhouettes and four weapon models.

## Combat presentation

- Muzzle flashes now use emissive material rather than a flat-colored primitive.
- Bullet tracers use bright emissive material for better readability against smoke, asphalt and the darker late-campaign lighting.
- Impact sparks use emissive material and a short expansion/contraction pulse instead of simply shrinking away.
- Manual lane aiming and HOLD FIRE behavior are unchanged.

## Bridge surface detail

- Added transparent layered road decals for tire skid marks, oil/grime, infected blood and burned/scorched asphalt.
- Decals are kept slightly above the road plane to avoid z-fighting.
- Standard and emissive materials now explicitly use anisotropic mipmapped texture filtering for cleaner angled surfaces such as the long road and bridge rails.

## Peripheral world additions

- Added a dedicated wrecked police-style SUV model with crushed body work, bent push bar, torn door, wheels and emergency-light breakup. It uses no real logos or trademarks.
- Added a large wrecked fire engine with modeled cab/body sections, wheels, ladder, hose reel, emergency lighting, smoke and burn treatment.
- Added reusable concrete rubble/rebar clusters around the bridge shoulders.
- New wreck sites include scorch decals and integrate with the existing v1.4 fire, smoke and ember animation system.
- Existing collapsed flyover, crashed helicopter, broken crane, quarantine booths, buses, ambulance, military trucks, containers, rooftop tanks, satellite dishes, utility hardware and ruined skyline remain in the scene.

## Reproducibility and licensing

All new v1.5 runtime art is original deterministic geometry/texture output generated by `tools/generate_graphics_v15.py`. No third-party model, texture, sound or font was added. The attempted external 3D generation path was not used because the connected generator account had no available balance; no external generated artifact is shipped or claimed.
